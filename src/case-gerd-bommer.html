Case study · Gerd Bommer

Untamed energy

Gerd Bommer spent 25 years building offices across Europe, the Middle East and North America. He invests in founders, speaks on stages and rides hard enduro. He had no brand that could hold all three, so we built one from strategy to motorbike.

About

Gerd Bommer is a founder-first investor, keynote speaker and strategic advisor. For more than 25 years he has helped companies enter new markets and scale across borders, working where startups, corporates, governments and capital meet. He has delivered over 500 talks and lectures.

Along the way he saw the people building the future burn out, and the most capable founders get overlooked. He decided to change how the game is played.

The brief

Gerd came with the career, the conviction and the energy, and no brand. There was nothing to refresh. Everything had to be built: the positioning, the name of each offer, the logo, the website and the content.

It also had to sound like him. An investor's brand usually whispers. Gerd does not.

Strategy

Investors measure cash, traction and KPIs. Gerd looks at the founder first: mindset, energy and the decisions they produce. That became the position. He is the founder-first investor, building a startup culture where “capital follows character, purpose and profitability”.

The website states who it is for in its first line: “For those bold enough to build what's next.”

Mindset is capital. Energy is currency.

The line and the G

The line puts the founder's inner state into the language of finance, which is the language his audience already speaks.

Energy also names the offers, and each name ends on his initial. Keynotes are The EnerG Talks. Workshops are The SynerG Labs. Strategic advising is The StrateG Sessions. Even the news section is called EnerG News.

Logo

The mark is a lightning bolt drawn as one outlined stroke. It stands beside his name in heavy capitals, where the R and the D share a stem and the B is cut open.

There are two colours, a deep royal blue and a sharp yellow, and they swap places freely. The bolt works alone as a bullet point, a favicon and a sticker.

Speaking T-shirts

Most speakers put their message on a slide. Gerd wears his. We branded his keynote outfit with a set of T-shirts in the brand blue, each carrying one line in yellow: “Your mindset is part of your business model.” “Decisions scale. Intensity doesn't.”

The line is in every photo taken of him on stage, so the audience's pictures carry the message for him.

The bike

Gerd rode Red Bull Romaniacs, the hard enduro rally in Romania. His motorbike went in brand blue with the bolt on the side panel and three lines in yellow: “Untamed energy. Rooted in purpose.” “Mature minds. Young hearts.” And on the front fender, where he sees it: “Get ready for the grind.”

A brand about energy should hold up in the mud.

Website

The website is blue from top to bottom. It introduces Gerd in three words, Investor, Keynote Speaker, Strategic Advisor, and gives each role its own page and its own offer.

The portrait that carries the About section shows him in mid-air, in jeans and a T-shirt. The speaker page lists three keynotes, among them “Mindset before skillset. Purpose before process.” and “Funding the female future.”

Social

On LinkedIn, Gerd teaches founders what investors look for. We built a template that is recognisable before a word is read: a blue headline with one phrase swiped in yellow, and a cartoon in the middle of the page.

There are two characters. One is Gerd. The other is the founder he is talking to. Both wear the speaking T-shirt, and the shirt carries the title of the post.

Result

Gerd now has one brand for the investor, the speaker and the rider. The same blue, the same bolt and the same lines appear on his website, in his feed, on his chest and on his bike.
